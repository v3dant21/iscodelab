Your data and choices

Notice version: 7 November 2026. This operational notice is not a certification of DPDP compliance.

Data used to serve your order

We store your name, email and contact phone number, delivery address, selected location, ordered items, bills and order history. Passwords are hashed. Contact details, order contact/address payloads, invoice snapshots and queued email recipients are encrypted in the database; keyed hashes support email and phone matching. The service decrypts the necessary details for you and authorised staff. We use these to authenticate you, select a kitchen, fulfil and track orders, provide bills and suggest dishes you have previously ordered. Suggestions never add items to your bag.

Order updates are separate from offers

A contact phone number is required before ordering so the kitchen can reach you; it is not OTP-verified. Choosing WhatsApp or SMS and confirming the order-update checkbox permits us to send messages about that order through the configured messaging service. Until that service is connected, messages are shown in the app and remain queued. No marketing permission is inferred from placing an order.

Email receipts

Email/password accounts must verify their email before we send order information to that address. Confirmed orders queue a tracking link and invoice PDF. Links require account sign-in. Until an email provider is connected, use in-app tracking and PDF downloads. Email delivery uses a separate provider; service updates do not opt you into marketing.

Rewards and party requests

We keep a points ledger linked to your orders to award and redeem rewards. Party requests use your profile contact details, venue, event time, guest count and menu or dietary preferences for review by an authorised catering manager. Party details, manager replies, cancellation reasons and rider references are encrypted. Party decisions appear in the app; submitting a request does not grant marketing consent.

Marketing consent

Marketing is off by default. You may opt in or withdraw in Account → Privacy & messages at any time. We record the choice, notice version and time. No marketing sender is currently connected.

Access and protection

Customers can access only their own orders and bills. Kitchen access is limited by outlet, and manager estimate changes are audited. Do not enter payment-card details in this app; the live payment gateway is not yet connected.

Before launch

The operator must publish its legal identity and privacy/grievance contact, specify approved retention periods and rights-request procedures, confirm processor arrangements, and complete the applicable security and legal review. These details are pending; this notice must be completed before collecting live customer data.
