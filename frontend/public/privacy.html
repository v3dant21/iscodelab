Your data and choices

Notice version: 6 November 2026. This operational notice is not a certification of DPDP compliance.

Data used to serve your order

We store your email, account credentials in hashed form, delivery address, selected location, ordered items, bills and order history. We use these to authenticate you, select a kitchen, fulfil and track orders, provide bills and suggest dishes you have previously ordered. Suggestions never add items to your bag.

Order updates are separate from offers

Phone numbers are optional. Choosing WhatsApp or SMS and confirming the order-update checkbox permits us to send messages about that order through the configured messaging service. Until that service is connected, messages are shown in the app and remain queued. No marketing permission is inferred from placing an order.

Marketing consent

Marketing is off by default. You may opt in or withdraw in Account → Privacy & messages at any time. We record the choice, notice version and time. No marketing sender is currently connected.

Access and protection

Customers can access only their own orders and bills. Kitchen access is limited by outlet, and manager estimate changes are audited. Do not enter payment-card details in this app; the live payment gateway is not yet connected.

Before launch

The operator must publish its legal identity and privacy/grievance contact, specify approved retention periods and rights-request procedures, confirm processor arrangements, and complete the applicable security and legal review. These details are pending; this notice must be completed before collecting live customer data.
